# Twenty-species original-art brief

Frozen order: `Catalog.ACTIVE_IDS` / `species_for_index(0..19)`. Existing `species_for_kind(0..7)` remains a separate legacy mapping; never reinterpret old numeric kinds as new indices. Full bilingual/scientific names remain in fish_catalog.gd. Selection is regionally familiar North American freshwater fish, including stocked trout and landlocked striped bass; not all share one natural lake.

This is an anatomy reference brief for **new original drawings**, not permission to copy source images. State-agency pages often credit copyrighted artists, and TPWD explicitly marks illustrations © TPWD. Government hosting does not automatically make state material public domain. No source photograph, illustration, traced outline, or downloaded image is supplied as a game asset. Facts and independently drawn anatomy may guide original art. Any later third-party image use needs separate license verification.

Adult side-profile art is the target. Sex, season, age and water alter color; do not claim every individual has identical colors. Distinct silhouette, mouth, fin placement and markings should survive downscaling. Fin counts are anatomical constraints, not necessarily every ray visible in tiny gameplay sprites. Dimensions and exaggeration are artist choices, not biological measurements.

| 2 black_crappie / 黑莓鲈 | Deep but longer oval than bluegill; large slightly upturned mouth; tall dorsal and broad anal fin; 7–8 dorsal spines; shallow fork tail. | Irregular peppered black patches on silver/olive; speckled fins. | 3 yellow_perch / 黄鲈 | Moderately slender, arched back; relatively small terminal mouth; clearly separate spiny and soft dorsal fins; forked tail. | Bold dark vertical bars on yellow/olive sides, orange lower fins. | 5 walleye / 大眼梭鲈 | Long tapered perch shape; large reflective eye and toothed terminal mouth; separated dorsal fins; forked tail. | Dark rear patch on first dorsal, white lower tail-lobe tip, olive-gold flank. Do not draw a pike duckbill. | [Michigan DNR](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/education/michigan-species/fish/walleye), [Minnesota DNR](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/fish/walleye/biology.html) |
| 6 northern_pike / 白斑狗鱼 | Very elongated torpedo, flattened duckbill snout; dorsal set far back opposite anal fin; forked tail. | Light bean/oval spots on dark olive flank, patterned yellowish fins. | 7 smallmouth_bass / 小口黑鲈 | Muscular elongated oval; closed jaw does not pass rear eye; shallower dorsal notch than largemouth; rounded/notched tail. | Bronze/olive with vertical side bars, dark cheek streaks. No continuous largemouth lateral band. | [TPWD identification](https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/fishing/freshwater-fishing/bass-identification), [species account](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/smb/) |
| 8 spotted_bass / 斑点黑鲈 | Bass form but moderate mouth, closed upper jaw not beyond eye; connected dorsal with shallower notch than largemouth; slightly notched tail. | Broken lateral dark band plus ordered rows of little dark belly-side dots. Not merely a recolored largemouth. | [TPWD identification](https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/fishing/freshwater-fishing/bass-identification), [species account](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/spb/) |
| 9 white_bass / 白鲈 | Deep compressed silver body, high shoulder, two separate dorsal fins and forked tail; smaller/shorter outline than striped bass. | Faint narrow horizontal lines; usually only central stripe runs fully toward tail. | 10 striped_bass / 条纹鲈 | Long, relatively slender silver fusiform body; two separated dorsals; strongly forked tail; terminal mouth. | Several distinct parallel horizontal stripes extend along body to tail region; stripe geometry and long outline matter more than hue. | 11 rock_bass / 岩钝鲈 | Stocky elongated panfish, larger mouth than bluegill; connected dorsal; anal fin with six spines; broad shallow-notched tail. | Red eye, olive-brown checker-like rows of dark side spots. Retain large jaw and red iris, not bluegill's tiny mouth/ear-spot shorthand. | [Michigan DNR](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/education/michigan-species/fish-species/rock-bass), [West Virginia DNR](https://wvdnr.gov/plants-animals/sportfish/rock-bass/) |
| 12 white_crappie / 白莓鲈 | Deep compressed but somewhat longer body than black crappie; upturned large mouth; dorsal 5–6 spines, high soft dorsal; shallow-fork tail. | Dark spots organized into vertical bars on silver sides; fins spotted. Distinguish by pattern arrangement and dorsal outline, not white versus black paint alone. | [TPWD species account](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/wcp/), [crappie guide](https://tpwd.texas.gov/publications/pwdpubs/media/pwd_br_t3200_178.pdf) |
| 13 green_sunfish / 绿太阳鱼 | Longer, heavier bass-like panfish body; conspicuously large mouth; connected dorsal and rounded pectoral; shallow-notched tail. | Blue-green cheek streaks/spangles, faint side bars, dark pale-edged ear flap; pale/yellow fin edges. Do not reuse deep-round bluegill body and tiny jaw. | [TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/greensunfish/), [Minnesota DNR](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/fish/sunfish/biology.html) |
| 14 redear_sunfish / 红耳太阳鱼 | Deep compressed sunfish; small mouth; long pointed pectoral, continuous dorsal; normal short rounded ear flap, not longear appendage. | Olive/silver mottling and distinct red/orange rim on black ear flap; less ornate cheek than pumpkinseed. Keep the ear short. | [TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/sunfish/) |
| 15 longear_sunfish / 长耳太阳鱼 | Deep compact oval; small mouth; continuous dorsal; conspicuously elongated dark opercular flap with pale border. | Adult male option: orange/red belly, turquoise cheek/fins and flecks. The long white-edged ear is essential; don't confuse with a small red ear-tip spot. | [TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/longearsunfish/) |
| 16 channel_catfish / 斑点叉尾鮰 | Scaleless tapering body, broad blunt head, visible barbels; triangular dorsal plus separate small adipose fin; deeply forked tail; curved anal margin. | Smaller adult/juvenile may have scattered dark flank spots; olive/slate, pale underside. Anal fin 24–29 rays; spots may disappear with age, so outline is primary. | [TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/ccf/), [comparison guide](https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/fishing/freshwater-fishing/catfish-identification) |
| 17 blue_catfish / 蓝鲶 | Broad-headed robust scaleless catfish with barbels, dorsal plus adipose; deeply forked tail; longer straight-edged anal fin. | Usually unspotted slate-blue/gray, pale belly; anal fin generally 30–35 rays. Do not distinguish only by blue coloration: straight anal edge versus channel's curved edge. | [TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/huntwild/wild/species/blc/), [comparison guide](https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/fishing/freshwater-fishing/catfish-identification) |
| 18 brook_trout / 溪红点鲑 | Slender trout/char form; moderate terminal jaw; one soft dorsal plus small rear adipose; near-square/slightly notched tail. | Pale wormlike back markings, red side spots with blue halos, pale spots on dark body; orange lower fins with white leading edges and dark inner border. No pink horizontal rainbow band. | [Wisconsin DNR](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/fishing/trout/BrookTroutReserves), [Michigan DNR](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/education/michigan-species/fish-species/brook-trout) |
| 19 rainbow_trout / 虹鳟 | Streamlined spindle; moderate mouth; one soft dorsal plus adipose; shallow fork/squared tail depending age. | Pink/rose lateral band on silver/olive flank; small dark spots over back and both tail lobes. Avoid brook's vermiculations, haloed red dots and white/orange fin-edge package. | [Michigan DNR](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/education/michigan-species/fish-species/rainbow-trout) |

## Original-art acceptance

- Exactly 20 complete assets, file names keyed by stable ID, with transparent backgrounds and coherent art direction.
- Review at intended small gameplay size and larger catalog size, not only enlarged contact sheets.
- Compare easily confused pairs explicitly: bluegill/pumpkinseed/redear/longear; three black bass; white/striped bass; white/black crappie; blue/channel catfish; brook/rainbow trout.
- Preserve actual mouth and fin positions in sprite transforms; do not mirror text baked into art (there should be none).
- The two catfish require adipose fins and barbels; trout require adipose fins; pike requires rear dorsal placement; perch/walleye/temperate bass require two separate dorsals.
- Availability changes only after integrated assets, index mapping, collision/hook/catch rendering and ledger IDs are verified together. A file merely existing is not acceptance.

Sources researched 2026-11-09. Full names and taxonomy caveats in fish-catalog.md; this document describes typical visual identifiers, not a legal fish-identification field guide.
